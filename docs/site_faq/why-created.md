# Why Created

Clew was created because the available tools did not match the actual shape of structured learning.

## The Personal Need

The product started from a simple need: a subject should stay visible as a structure instead of collapsing into scattered notes, vague plans, or generic chat history.

The workspace was built to be useful in real study, not only as a concept demo.

## Agent Products Needed Boundaries

I did not want an AI tool that quietly changes important study structure behind the scenes.

Clew keeps the job narrow: help a learner build, inspect and revise a path through a subject.

## Learning Needed Its Own Workspace

Structured learning needs dependency visibility, clear branching, auditability, progress tied to meaning, and AI that helps without taking control away.

Clew exists because those needs deserve a dedicated workspace instead of being squeezed into note apps, whiteboards, or chat windows.

## Why The Graph Matters

The graph is not a gimmick. A graph is the most honest representation of a subject when some topics depend on others, several paths are possible, and the learner wants to understand the shape of the field.

That is why the graph became the product center instead of an optional visualization layer.

## Why Clew Is Worth Making

Students, researchers, self-directed builders, and people jumping into new technical fields quickly do not just need more content. They need better structure and better control over how that structure evolves.

Clew exists to make that structure visible, editable, AI-assisted, and still owned by the learner.
